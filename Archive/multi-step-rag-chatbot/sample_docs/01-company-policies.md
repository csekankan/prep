# Acme Cloud — Company Policies (Sample)

> **Fictional document for learning RAG.** Your chatbot ingests this file and answers questions from it.

## Remote work

All engineers may work remotely up to **3 days per week**. Managers must approve a **4th remote day** per quarter. Core collaboration hours are **10:00–16:00** in the employee's local timezone.

## PTO and holidays

- **PTO:** 20 days per year, accrued monthly. Unused PTO rolls over up to **5 days** into the next year.
- **Sick leave:** 10 days per year; no rollover.
- **Company holidays:** Listed in the internal HR portal; US offices observe 10 fixed holidays.

## Expense policy

Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to **$75 per day**. Client entertainment requires **pre-approval** from a director if the expected cost exceeds **$500**.

## Security

- Laptops must use **full-disk encryption** and a screen lock under **5 minutes**.
- **Customer data** must never be stored on personal devices.
- Report suspected incidents to **security@acme-cloud.example** within **1 hour**.

## Refunds (customer-facing, internal summary)

Support may issue refunds without manager approval when:

1. The charge is within **30 days**, and
2. The amount is under **$200**, and
3. The customer has **fewer than 3** prior refunds in 12 months.

Larger refunds require **Tier-2 approval** and a ticket link in the CRM.
